Model and constants

Calculation chain (as in the workbook): mion = matom − Q·me; E0 = 931.481252 MeV/u · mion; βSSC from ESSC; forb,SSC = β c / 26.1129 m; fRF = hSSC·forb,SSC; hSPC2 = hSSC/2; βSPC2 = 2.99240 m · fRF/hSPC2 / c; ESPC2 = (γSPC2 − 1)E0.

Harmonic rule: hSSC = 4 if 4·forb lies in 8–26 MHz, otherwise 12 if 12·forb does; outside both, 12 (too low) or 4 (too high) with a warning.

SPC2 orbits: geometry A (Rinj 23.73 mm, N 33–36), B (33.75 mm, 16–19), C (48.85 mm, 8–11). Einj = ½ m (2π forb Rinj)², valid if 2Q ≤ Einj ≤ 20Q keV. Vd = (ESPC2 − Einj)/(4 N Q). Optimum = feasible geometry with the highest Vd ≤ 60 kV; Vd > 54 kV flags sparking risk and a safe alternative (Vd ≤ 52 kV) is offered. Orbit radius r = β c / (2π forb,SPC2); extraction radius 476.3 mm.

SSC orbits: hard-edge model, 4 sector magnets of 34°, radial edges, field-free valleys; orbit = 90° arc of radius ρ in each magnet + straight valley sections. ρ scales with β (isochronous), fixed so that the extraction path length is 26.1129 m; this gives r = 1.015 m (magnet centre) at injection and 4.428 m at extraction. With the magnets at 45°, 135°, 225° and 315°, the two 49° delta dees sit in the valleys at 90° and 270° (gaps at 65.5°, 114.5°, 245.5°, 294.5°); the beam runs clockwise by default. Turns N = ceil((ESSC − ESPC2)/ΔEmax), then ΔE = (ESSC − ESPC2)/N.

Drawing: within each turn the energy rises linearly with azimuth (the workbook's smooth acceleration model) and every turn gains the same ΔE; the orbit is the equilibrium orbit of the instantaneous energy. Injection and extraction are both at the extraction azimuth. In the sector graph the azimuth axis always increases to the right, so a clockwise beam travels from right to left and leaves at the left edge.

Main-field ripple: a variation δ = ΔB/B of the whole SSC field (main coils in series, ripple periods of ms against an acceleration time of tens of µs, so δ is constant during one acceleration) shifts the orbital frequency by δ while the RF stays fixed. The RF phase then slips by 2π·h·δ per turn, the energy gain follows cos φ, so after t turns E(t) = Einj + ΔE·sin(2πhδt)/(2πhδ), and the equilibrium radius at that energy is rEO(E)/(1+δ). The band is the envelope of the orbit over δ from −δpp/2 to +δpp/2; its width at the extraction azimuth is the quoted broadening. The current conversion uses ΔI = I·δpp; the default current is a linear estimate from the 1600 A, 1.3 T main-coil supply rating and the hard-edge hill field at extraction. Trim-coil supplies are not included.
