SSC Injection Bunchers

The drift-tube bunchers in the injection line between the injector cyclotron and the SSC, iThemba LABS. Opens with ⁴He¹⁺ at 28.5 MeV, a 20° bunch from the injector, and a 25 m line with the 4fRF buncher at 10 m and the 2fRF buncher at 15 m. Set the length of the line, one or two bunchers, their frequencies and positions, then watch one bunch travel the line and see its length at the start, at each buncher and at the centre of the SSC.

Optimiser
Searches the voltage (0 to the limit) and the phase of each buncher, keeping the mean energy of the bunch on the injection energy. "Most beam within the pulse window" maximises the share of the beam inside the best window of the given length at the centre of the SSC; "shortest bunch" minimises the rms length. With two bunchers and the limit applied, buncher 1 must keep the bunch short enough at buncher 2.
